Transparency & Data Safety

MS Student Library creates a dedicated folder in your Google Drive named "MS Student Library" to store your backups. We do not access your private files or share your personal data with any third party.

Your data belongs to you. You can manage, switch, or remove your connected accounts at any time directly from the app settings.
