Data Safety & Transparency

MS Student Library creates a dedicated folder in your personal Google Drive named "MS Student Library". This space is used exclusively for your personal backups. We do not access your private files, and your data is never stored on our servers.

You have full control over your accounts and data. You can switch or remove connected accounts instantly within the app settings.
