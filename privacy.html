Privacy Policy for MS Student Library

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

We respect your privacy. MS Student Library uses Google Drive API solely to backup and sync your personal study documents.

Data Collection

The app only accesses files that it creates or that you specifically select. We do not see your private photos or other Drive data.

Data Usage

We do not store your data on our own servers. All backups are stored securely in your own Google account.
